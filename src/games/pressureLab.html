Welcome to Pressure Lab

An interactive virtual physics laboratory designed for Rwanda S1–S2 Secondary School. Explore how hydrostatic pressure increases with depth, learn Archimedes' theory of buoyancy, and crack the mechanical secrets of Pascal's Hydraulic Press!

Stage 1

Pressure Under Water

Drag sensors inside depth zones and add metal overload blocks to observe proportional pressures. Link the fluid depth hydrostatic rule to fundamental forces.

Stage 2

Archimedes' Buoyancy

Suspend test cubes into a water tank using a visual spring force scale. Watch the scale tension scale compress as buoyant forces rise. Study density releases!

Stage 3

Pascal's Hydraulic Jack

Build mechanical pressure transfers to multiply piston outputs. Conduct float/sink tests on floating blocks to check conditions.

Rwanda S1–S2 Pressure & Buoyancy Exploration Course Certificate of Merit

Pressure
P = F / A
Pressure is forced concentrated over a local cross section area, measured in Pascals (Pa).
Hydro Depth
P = ρ · g · h
Hydrostatic water pressure is directly proportional to depth height, water density and gravity.
Buoyancy
Fb = ρ · g · V_sub
Buoyancy force matches the exact gravity weight of the liquid displaced (Archimedes).
Pascal's Law
F1/A1 = F2/A2
Confined force distributes uniform pressure. Modest inlet forces lift immense heavy vehicles.
