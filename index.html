How to Bulk Import

Step 1: Download the Template
Click the green "Download Template" button to get a pre-formatted CSV file.

Step 2: Fill in your Data
Add your resource titles, topics, grades, and the exact filenames of your HTML exercises. For icons, you can type a local filename (e.g., icon.png).

Step 3: Import the Data
Upload your saved CSV/Excel file using the "Import Data" button.

Step 4: Upload the Assets (ZIP)
Highlight your actual HTML files and images on your computer, compress them into a .zip file, and upload them via the "Bulk Upload Assets" tool below. The server will sort everything into the correct folders automatically!
